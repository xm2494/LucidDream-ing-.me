FILM SYNOPSIS

This film was filmed and takes place inside of the Arts Center, particularly inside of the empty hallways.

The main story/narrative behind the film was about a girl wandering inside of the empty hallways and staircases with subtle signalling that this may not be a real event.

For instance, at the beginning of the film, the girl is with her friend, who then promptly disappears which siganls that something is off about this environment.

Furthermore, as the girl progresses throughtout her exploration, she begins loosing items on her over time.

First it's the black bag. Next the glasses are gone. At last, the watch is missing.

All of this occur throughout the film, with the climax being when the girl stumbles across a workshop related room and opens a cabinet that had a label saying "To NOT touch".

Bang! The girl turns around, squints, before running away in fear before she wakes up.

Everything is there. Except for the watch. So was it really all just a dream?

In hindsight, the watch was meant to be significant due to fact that time does not flow linearly and watches get distorted in dreams
